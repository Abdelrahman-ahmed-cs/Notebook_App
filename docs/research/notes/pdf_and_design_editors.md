# PDF-annotation and page-design editors: feature inventory (Xodo, Drawboard PDF, Flexcil, Acrobat Reader, PDF-XChange Editor, Canva)

Method note: this was researched on 2026-10-09. The sandbox egress proxy refused direct page fetches (CONNECT 403) for canva.com, support.drawboard.com, apps.microsoft.com and similar hosts. Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Treat exact menu labels and orderings as approximate. Items marked "Inference" are not sourced and are the researcher's own judgment.

## Which PDF annotation features are standard expectations in 2025–2026 for Windows users?

### Takeaway
The baseline that free tools set, and so what users expect, is: highlight, underline and strikethrough snapped to PDF text; sticky notes and comments with a comment list; freehand ink; shapes and arrows; text boxes; stamps; form filling; and draw/type/image signatures. Acrobat Reader and Xodo give all of these away free. The PDF-XChange Editor free tier adds measurement (distance, perimeter, area), custom stamps and basic OCR. The "organize" set (rotate, crop, split, merge, rearrange), plus flatten, compress and OCR, is common but is often paid (Xodo Pro or web tools, PDF-XChange Plus or PRO, Drawboard Pro).

### Cited Findings
**Adobe Acrobat Reader (free baseline)**
- Reader is free with no subscription. Adobe lists view, print, annotate, fill forms and sign as free on desktop and mobile. Editing and export need paid Acrobat Pro. — [Toolradar, Best Free PDF Reader 2026](https://toolradar.com/blog/best-free-pdf-reader); [Adobe annotate how-to](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/how-to/annotate-pdf-online.html)
- The Reader annotation set is sticky notes, typed text, highlight, underline, strikethrough and freehand drawing. — [Trinity University LibGuide](https://libguides.trinity.edu/digital-annotation/adobe); [Erin Wright Writing](https://erinwrightwriting.com/comment-and-markup-tools-in-adobe-acrobat/)
- Sticky note workflow: pick the tool, click a spot on the page, then type in the Comment pane. — [Chron](https://smallbusiness.chron.com/add-comments-pdf-acrobat-reader-55336.html)
- The Stamp tool has preset stamps (Approved, Confidential, Sign Here) in Dynamic, Sign Here and Standard Business categories. — [thesoftwarepro.com](https://thesoftwarepro.com/acrobat-comments/); [Acrobat XI how-to PDF (old)](https://www.basearts.com/curriculum/ADOBE/Acrobat/AXI_howto_use_comment_tools.pdf)
- Fill & Sign detects whether a PDF has fillable fields. A signature can be drawn, typed, or uploaded as an image. — [Adobe online editor](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/online/pdf-editor.html); [UPenn Law](https://www.law.upenn.edu/its/docs/adobe/acrobat-review.php)
- One older guide says Reader allows sticky notes and highlights only if the author enabled Reader commenting. That restriction is probably outdated (from the Acrobat XI era). — [Chron](https://smallbusiness.chron.com/add-comments-pdf-acrobat-reader-55336.html)

**Xodo (Windows Store app, web tools, PDF Studio desktop)**
- Microsoft Store listing, free tier: comments, sticky notes, strikethrough, links, highlight, underline, an "Annotation summary" view of all annotations, and shapes (lines, arrows, circles, rectangles). Pro tier: image, page and text stamps, and flattening or removing annotations. — [Microsoft Store via search summary](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9WZDNCRDJXP4)
- Winget lists Xodo PDF Reader at v2025.3.0, with newer 2026.0.0–2026.1.2 builds in its version archive. Features listed: add text, comments, notes, highlights, freehand drawing. — [wingetgui.com Xodo PDF Reader](https://wingetgui.com/apps/Apryse-XodoPDFReader)
- Xodo PDF Studio (Windows, macOS, Linux; paid) is an offline editor with editing, annotation, conversion and OCR. Its batch processor can "merge, OCR, sign, secure, and optimize hundreds of PDFs". — [wingetgui Xodo PDF Studio](https://www.wingetgui.com/apps/Apryse-XodoPDFStudio); [xodo.com/pdf-studio](https://xodo.com/pdf-studio); [Introducing Xodo PDF Studio](https://xodo.com/blog/introducing-new-xodo-pdf-studio)
- Xodo web tools: Crop, Compress, Flatten, OCR converter, plus an Organize group (Split, Merge, Rotate, Rearrange). — [xodo.com/tools](https://xodo.com/tools)
- Xodo mobile: rotate and crop; add, remove and rearrange pages; split and extract. Flatten "combine[s] all the existing layers (graphics, text, annotations, and form fields) into a single unified layer". Compression has Low and High settings. OCR gives "searchable text". — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.xodo.pdf.reader&hl=en_US); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pdf-reader-editor-xodo/id805075929)
- Older Windows release notes (undated) mention sound annotation playback (free), sound recording (Pro), and a fix for editing text-markup annotations. — [onmsft.com](https://onmsft.com/?p=43623)

**PDF-XChange Editor (Windows power-user reference)**
- Free tier: perimeter and area measurement tools, comment tools (shapes, text boxes, hyperlinks, audio), and custom stamps. — [PDF-XChange features page](https://www.pdf-xchange.com/product/pdf-xchange-editor/features-group/108/features); [V11 manual features overview](https://help.pdf-xchange.com/pdfxt11/features-overview.html)
- Measurement preferences include snapping to grid and guides. Snapping sensitivity can be set per annotation type, and snap points are selectable. — [PDF-XChange V11 manual](https://help.pdf-xchange.com/pdfxt11/features-overview.html)
- OCR: the free Editor has a basic engine. "Enhanced OCR" is PRO-only, and reviews say Plus uses ABBYY. — [Products comparison chart](https://www.pdf-xchange.com/pdf-xchange-products-comparison-chart); [pdnob review 2026](https://www.pdnob.com/alternative/pdf-xchange-reviews.html); [QSCD review](https://www.qscd.eu/tips-tricks/our-review-of-pdf-xchange-editor/)
- Conflict on dynamic stamps: the vendor chart lists custom dynamic stamps as free, while a review says creating and editing dynamic stamps is Editor Plus only. — [Comparison chart](https://www.pdf-xchange.com/pdf-xchange-products-comparison-chart) vs [pdnob review](https://www.pdnob.com/alternative/pdf-xchange-reviews.html)
- Conflict on licensing model: one source says the free build includes the pro features but watermarks output until registered. The vendor's chart instead splits features between free and licensed. — [FileHorse](https://www.filehorse.com/download-pdf-xchange-editor/) vs [Comparison chart](https://www.pdf-xchange.com/pdf-xchange-products-comparison-chart)

**Drawboard PDF**
- The Store listing includes PDF bookmarks, print, PDF search, markup history and share. — [Microsoft Store (drawboard)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/p/drawboard-pdf-read-edit-annotate-pdf/9wzdncrfhwqt)
- Calibration to scale, plus linear and area measurement. The Store listing describes calibration as Pro. — [Drawboard blog: measurement and angles](https://www.drawboard.com/blog/add-measurement-and-angles-to-your-markups-using-drawboard-pdf); [Drawboard PDF Pro](https://www.drawboard.com/pdf/pro)
- Digital protractor (0–360°) and an on-page ruler are Pro. Grid and lined paper templates are Pro. "Templates and Overlays" are listed. — [Drawboard Pro page](https://www.drawboard.com/pdf/pro); [Drawboard vs Adobe](https://www.drawboard.com/compare/adobe)
- "Full functionality without internet access" (third-party listing). — [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Drawboard-PDF/)

### Inferences
- Must-have for an offline Electron and Fabric.js notebook to reach the Reader/Xodo-free baseline: text-snapped highlight, underline and strikethrough (needs a PDF.js text layer with char rects); sticky notes or comments with a sidebar list ("annotation summary"); a shape set with arrows; text boxes; ink; signature capture (draw, type, image) that is reusable; preset stamps; PDF text search; and bookmark/outline import as a navigation tree.
- Differentiators seen at power-user level (PDF-XChange, Drawboard Pro): calibrated measurement, ruler and protractor, snapping, custom stamps, and paper templates (grid, lined). Page organize tools (rotate, split, merge, insert from another PDF, reorder) are table stakes in Xodo and PDF-XChange, though often paywalled.
- Flatten vs editable on export: Xodo treats flatten as an explicit (Pro) action. That suggests the default export should keep annotations as real PDF annotation objects or editable layers, with flatten as an option. An app that only rasterizes or burns in its overlay is effectively "always flattened", which is a gap.
- OCR and compression can be done offline (for example tesseract.js or ghostscript/qpdf-style recompression), but full-quality OCR is the feature vendors charge for.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Acrobat Reader (free) includes the Measure tool in 2025–2026. No search result addressed it.
- I found no confirmation that Xodo Windows has measurement tools.
- Form-field types beyond "fills fillable fields" (checkbox, radio, dropdown, comb fields) and links behavior (internal "go to page" vs URL) were not detailed for any product.
- No 2025 Windows changelog was found for Xodo.
- Bookmarks and outline import were confirmed only for Drawboard (Store listing).

## How do Drawboard PDF and Xodo implement their Windows UI (radial menu, toolbar, pen presets)?

### Takeaway
Drawboard PDF is built around a draggable, collapsible Radial Menu (8 components: pan, a select category of rectangle, text, lasso and laser pointer, pen, highlighter, and so on). It can now be swapped for a linear "Advanced Tools Drawer" that reaches any tool option in two clicks, plus a Favorites/Markup Toolbar of user-saved pen presets. Xodo uses a conventional top toolbar with annotation tool groups and an "Annotation summary" panel. Detailed Xodo Windows UI documentation was not retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- The Radial Menu is "one of the most iconic and intuitive features of Drawboard PDF": all tools in one place, draggable and collapsible. Support documentation lists 8 components, including Pan, a Select category (rectangle, text, lasso, laser pointer) and Pen. — [Drawboard support: Components in the Radial Menu](https://support.drawboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/4414978334863-Radial-Menu)
- To edit pen properties, open the pen's arrow or outer ring in the radial menu. The highlighter works the same way. — [Drawboard support: Radial Menu](https://support.drawboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/4414978334863)
- The radial menu is toggled via Settings cog → View & Layout → "Use radial menu". — [Drawboard blog: best set-up](https://www.drawboard.com/blog/the-best-way-to-set-up-drawboard-pdf-to-increase-productivity)
- Advanced Tools Drawer: users choose either the Radial Menu or the drawer. Drawboard says a tool category and its options are reachable "in two clicks rather than up to 10". — [Drawboard blog: Advanced Tools Drawer](https://drawboard.com/blog/new-in-drawboard-pdf-the-advanced-tools-drawer)
- Pen presets: on Windows desktop, select the pen from the Favorites Toolbar and click its icon twice to open customization. Choose among pen presets with "different levels of digital inking control", or set color, thickness and opacity manually. — [Drawboard blog: how to draw on a PDF](https://www.drawboard.com/blog/how-to-draw-on-a-pdf-with-drawboard-pdf)
- Multiple presets: on the Markup Toolbar, press "+" then "+" on Pen, customize, and the preset is saved for reuse. — [Drawboard blog: top 6 productivity tips](https://drawboard.com/blog/top-6-productivity-tips)
- Historical context: the radial control came from a Microsoft/Drawboard collaboration for Surface (2015). — [Microsoft ISE devblog](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/ise/building-a-radial-menu-control-for-surface-devices/)
- Xodo Windows exposes an "Annotation summary" showing all annotations at once, and groups its shape tools (lines, arrows, circles, rectangles). — [Microsoft Store via search summary](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9WZDNCRDJXP4)
- Flexcil (a comparison point for pen UX; tablet-first): separate pen mode and gesture mode. Gestures include underline, round bracket, long-press, box, circle, "L", eraser gesture and undo/redo. The highlighter renders behind ink. The lasso can filter by object type (pen, highlight, image, textbox, or any combination). One reviewer says lasso selections can't be rotated or moved across pages. Shape-drawing snaps to other strokes. — [Flexcil gesture vs pen mode](https://www.flexcil.com/support/switch-between-gesture-mode-and-pen-mode/); [Flexcil 2 gestures](https://www.flexcil.com/support/gesture-introduction-2/); [Being Paperless review](https://beingpaperless.com/flexcil-notes-everything-you-need-to-know/); [Flexcil lasso help](https://www.flexcil.com/support/adding-textbox-image-and-using-lasso-tool-2/)
- Flexcil is described as a note-taking and PDF app for tablet and mobile devices. The results did not confirm a native Windows app. — [UPDF on Flexcil](https://updf.com/edit-pdf/flexcil/); [flexcil.com](https://www.flexcil.com/)

### Inferences
- For a pen-first Windows notebook, the strongest proven patterns are: (1) a user-built row of saved pen and highlighter presets (color, width, opacity, tip type), one click to switch and double-click to edit; (2) an optional floating radial palette for tablet and pen users; (3) a lasso that filters by object type; and (4) the highlighter drawn under ink (multiply blend or lower z-order).
- Drawboard's move from radial-only to a drawer suggests a radial menu should be optional, not the only UI.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve the full list of Drawboard's 8 radial components, or current (2026) Drawboard toolbar screenshots and order.
- Xodo Windows' detailed toolbar layout, pen preset model and keyboard shortcuts were not found.
- Whether Flexcil ships a Windows build in 2026 is unconfirmed.

## Exactly what text and image editing controls does Canva expose in its contextual toolbar, and in what order?

### Takeaway
When text is selected, Canva's top contextual toolbar exposes (roughly left to right): Font family, Font size (− field +), Text color, Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Case (aA), Alignment (cycles left, center, right, justify), List (bullets, numbers), Spacing (letter spacing and line height sliders), Effects, Animate, and Position, with Transparency, Lock, Copy style and Duplicate in the right-hand area or "…" overflow. With an image selected it exposes Edit (photo editor: adjust, filters, effects), Crop, Flip, Animate, Position, Transparency, Lock and Copy style. The exact ordering is not documented officially and changes between Canva releases, so only the membership is sourced.

### Cited Findings
**Text controls**
- Font size: pick a preset or type a value. +/− buttons adjust it. — [Canva Help: Format text](https://www.canva.com/help/format-text/)
- Bold, Italic, Underline and Strikethrough are toolbar buttons. Alignment covers Left, Center, Right and Justify. — [Design Bundles: text options](https://designbundles.net/design-school/how-to-use-text-options-in-canva); [Canva Help: Format text](https://www.canva.com/help/format-text/)
- The "aA" toolbar icon toggles uppercase and lowercase. Some guides describe an "All caps"/text case setting. The Uppercase shortcut is Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K. — [graphicdesignresource.com](https://graphicdesignresource.com/how-to-make-text-uppercase-in-canva/); [justclickhere.co.uk](https://justclickhere.co.uk/canva-keyboard-shortcuts/)
- A bullet-list icon is on the text toolbar. The Spacing button opens Letter spacing and Line spacing sliders. — [Design Bundles freshdesk](https://fontbundles.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/42000110399-how-to-format-text-in-canva)
- Bold is greyed out for fonts without a bold weight. A suggested workaround is the Outline effect. — [All Ages Academy](https://www.allagesacademy.com/blog/canva-text-bold-legibility-guide)
- Effects panel groups, per Canva's help center: Style (Drop [shadow], Glow, Echo, Outline, Background, Splice), Color (Hollow, Glitch, Neon) and Shape (Curve). Effects have sliders for intensity, color, direction and transparency. Neon, Hollow, Glitch and Splice may use only the first gradient color. "Lift" appears in third-party guides but not in the help center's category list. — [Canva Help: Enhance text with effects](https://www.canva.com/help/text-effects/); [Design Bundles: text effects](https://designbundles.net/design-school/how-to-use-canva-text-effects)
- Text highlight is done via the Background effect. — [MagicSlides: highlight text in Canva](https://www.magicslides.app/blog/how-to-highlight-text-in-canva)

**Image controls**
- Crop: select Crop on the editor toolbar and drag the crop handles. Double-clicking the image also enters crop. Sources disagree on whether Crop is a top-level button or a tab inside Edit Photo. — [Canva Help: Resize and crop](https://www.canva.com/help/resize-and-crop/); [designyourway](https://www.designyourway.net/blog/how-to-crop-in-canva/)
- Flip (Flip horizontal, Flip vertical) is in the top toolbar. — [LeadMonitor](https://leadmonitor.ai/canva/how-to-flip-an-image-in-canva/); [Canva CN: flip and rotate](https://www.canva.cn/en/help/flip-and-rotate-cn/)
- Transparency: a toolbar icon opens a slider plus a numeric field. — [Canva Help: Transparency](https://www.canva.com/help/transparency/)
- Edit Photo has Filters (with an Intensity slider), an Adjust tab (sliders for brightness, contrast and similar, with Reset) and preset looks. Some guides mention AI Smart Crop. — [Canva Help: Photo editor](https://www.canva.com/help/image-editor/); [Canva Help: Image settings](https://www.canva.com/help/image-settings/); [GuidingTech](https://www.guidingtech.com/how-to-edit-images-in-canva/)
- The Position button opens Arrange (Forward, To front, Backward, To back), Align (top, left, middle, center, bottom, right) and Space evenly or Tidy up, plus advanced Width, Height, X, Y and rotation fields with a ratio lock. The Layers tab lists elements, which can be dragged to reorder them. — [Canva Help: Resize and crop](https://www.canva.com/help/resize-and-crop/); [ANDIPEW: Canva layers guide](https://andipew.com/canva-layers-guide/); [Laisla Designs: Position tool and layers (2025)](https://www.laisladesigns.com/2025/08/12/canva-basics-part-1/)

**Layout aids and pages**
- Tidy Up rearranges selected elements to be evenly spaced and aligned. — [trupeer.ai](https://www.trupeer.ai/tutorials/how-to-evenly-space-elements-in-canva)
- Rulers and guides: File → Settings (or View settings) → "Show rulers and guides". Guides are dragged from the rulers, and elements snap to them. Alignment and smart guides appear while dragging. Sources disagree on the exact menu path. — [saltfish.ai](https://saltfish.ai/tutorials/canva/how-to-show-rulers-and-guides); [layerpath](https://www.layerpath.com/learn/how-to-show-ruler-on-canva); [inspiretips](https://inspiretips.blog/canva-ruler-guide-lines-39479)
- Pages: the Grid view icon is in the bottom corner. Duplicate page, Delete page and Add page are in the page toolbar or a page's "More" menu. Multi-select with Ctrl. Drag pages to reorder. Designs can have up to 350 pages. — [Canva Help: Manage pages](https://www.canva.com/help/manage-pages/); [Canva Help: Page view settings](https://www.canva.com/help/page-view-settings/)

### Inferences
- A Fabric.js equivalent of the text toolbar, in order: Font family (system fonts with preview) | Size − [n] + | Color | B I U S | aA case | Align | List | Spacing (letter spacing via `charSpacing`, line height via `lineHeight`) | Effects (shadow via `fabric.Shadow`, outline via `stroke`/`paintFirst`, background via `textBackgroundColor`; curve needs a custom path-text implementation) | Position | Transparency | Lock | Copy style | Duplicate | Delete. Text presets (Add heading, Add subheading, Add body text) belong in a left-side "Text" panel, as in Canva.
- The image toolbar should be: Replace | Crop (also on double-click) | Flip H/V | Filters/Adjust (Fabric image filters: Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Grayscale, Blur) | Corner radius (via clipPath) | Position | Transparency | Lock.
- Margins and bleed: Canva offers these ("Show margins", "Show print bleed") alongside rulers in its view settings. This comes from the researcher's prior knowledge and was not confirmed in this research.

### Gaps
- No official, current, ordered list of Canva's text or image toolbar buttons was retrievable. The order above is an inference.
- Not confirmed from sources: Copy style (paint roller) behavior, Brand Kit (saved colors, fonts, logos, styles), font combinations and text presets, Elements library contents (lines with arrowheads, frames, grids, stickers), image replace by drag-drop, rounded corners, comments, version and undo history, and margins and bleed toggles. Searches did not return these topics. They are well known Canva features but remain unsourced here.

## Which Canva shortcuts are most used?

### Takeaway
The core Canva shortcut vocabulary, consistent across many third-party lists, is: T (text box), R (rectangle), C (circle), L (line); Ctrl+D (duplicate, repeat with the same offset); Alt+drag (duplicate while dragging); Ctrl+G and Ctrl+Shift+G (group, ungroup); Ctrl+B, I and U; Ctrl+Shift+K (uppercase); Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z (undo, redo); Shift-constrained resize; arrow nudge (Shift for larger steps). The lock shortcut is disputed: Ctrl+Shift+L vs Alt+Shift+L.

### Cited Findings
- T adds a text box, R a rectangle, C a circle, L a line. — [bloggingguide.com](https://bloggingguide.com/canva-keyboard-shortcuts/); [Jennifer Maker](https://jennifermaker.com/canva-keyboard-shortcuts/)
- Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicates. The copy lands offset, and repeating it keeps the last move's offset, which gives evenly spaced copies. — [keyshortcuts.net (2026)](https://keyshortcuts.net/blog/canva-shortcuts); [Fallon Travels](https://fallontravels.com/blog/canva-keyboard-shortcuts)
- Alt/Option+drag makes a quick copy while dragging. — [keyshortcuts.net](https://keyshortcuts.net/canva-shortcuts); [shortcuts.kstanchev.com](https://shortcuts.kstanchev.com/apps/canva/)
- Ctrl/Cmd+G groups and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+G ungroups. — [Sonary](https://sonary.com/content/how-to-group-elements-on-canva/); [Laisla Designs 2025 full list](https://www.laisladesigns.com/2025/04/25/cava-hacks-part-6-canva-keyboard-shortcuts-full-list/)
- Lock conflict: some lists give Ctrl+Shift+L, others Alt+Shift+L (Option+Shift+L on Mac). One source says to use the toolbar lock icon instead. — [keyshortcuts.net](https://keyshortcuts.net/canva-shortcuts) vs [shortcut-tools.com](https://shortcut-tools.com/en/shortcuts/canva/); [Boosterthon cheat sheet PDF (Oct 2025)](https://boosterthon.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CANVA-TIPS-SHORTCUTS.pdf)
- Text shortcuts: Ctrl+B bold, Ctrl+I italic, Ctrl+U underline, Ctrl+Shift+K uppercase. — [justclickhere.co.uk: 17 text shortcuts](https://justclickhere.co.uk/canva-keyboard-shortcuts/)
- I found no keyboard shortcut for align. Alignment is done through the Position panel or Tidy up. — [Sonary](https://sonary.com/content/how-to-group-elements-on-canva/)
- Context-dependent: in presentation mode, C triggers confetti. — [Jennifer Maker](https://jennifermaker.com/canva-keyboard-shortcuts/)
- Canva's official shortcut page is at canva.com/help/keyboard-shortcuts. It is organized into basic, element, text, and other sections. Its content was not retrievable. — [Canva Help: Keyboard shortcuts](https://www.canva.com/help/keyboard-shortcuts/)

### Inferences
- Recommended minimum shortcut set for the notebook app, with Canva parity: T, R, C, L; Ctrl+D; Alt+drag; Ctrl+G and Ctrl+Shift+G; Ctrl+Shift+L for lock (the more common listing); Ctrl+B, I and U; Ctrl+Shift+K; Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+Shift+Z; Delete; arrows (1px) and Shift+arrows (10px); Shift while dragging (constrain axis or aspect); Ctrl+]/[ and Ctrl+Alt+]/[ for layer order; Ctrl+A; Ctrl+C, X and V; and a "?" or Ctrl+/ overlay listing all shortcuts. The layer-order and "?" keys come from the researcher's prior knowledge of Canva and were not confirmed in this research.
- In Fabric.js, Ctrl+D with "repeat last offset" behavior means storing the delta between the last duplicate and its source.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed from sources: the official Canva list for layer ordering (Ctrl+] etc.), zoom (Ctrl+±, Ctrl+0), shift-nudge distance, text-size shortcuts (Ctrl+Shift+, and .), and line-height and letter-spacing shortcuts. The official page could not be fetched.
- The correct current lock shortcut is unresolved (Ctrl+Shift+L vs Alt+Shift+L).
