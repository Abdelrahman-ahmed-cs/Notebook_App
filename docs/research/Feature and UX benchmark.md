# Win Windows with native, offline, iPad-grade ink

The #1 spot for notebooks on Windows is open. No vendor ships a **native, offline, page-based notebook with GoodNotes-class ink, lasso and page management**. GoodNotes for Windows is called a "half-baked" web port that can't open documents offline. OneNote is free but has a history of ink lag and isn't page-first. Samsung Notes locked non-Galaxy PCs out of users' own notes from October 2023 until about May 2025. Notability, CollaNote, Flexcil and ZoomNotes have no real Windows app. The current plan (PDF import, paper templates, page operations, Canva-style text, images, shapes, smart guides, pressure ink, undo, zip save, PDF/PNG export, dark mode) covers the *design-editor* half of the category and the basics of ink. It leaves out the features that decide the *notebook* half. Those are a full lasso suite (type filter, recolor, cross-page move, screenshot, save-as-sticker), three eraser modes with an erase filter, a saved pen-preset row, text-snapped PDF highlight and underline, a deep page sidebar (bookmarks, outline, change template, insert-between), and PDF export that stays editable and compact. The loudest user complaints are subscription paywalls on features people already paid for, laggy web ports, vendor lock-in, and exports that balloon 6–8×. Each can be answered by architecture rather than by adding features: offline-first, local open files, honest one-time pricing, and built-in compression. The professional look comes from Windows 11 Fluent restraint: neutral grey layers, 4px/8px radii, the Segoe UI Variable ramp, one accent colour, 20px Fluent icons, shadows only on floating surfaces, and the white paper page as the visual hero. One caveat: most competitor facts come from search-engine summaries of help pages and reviews, because the research sandbox couldn't fetch vendor sites directly. Competitors don't publish their toolbar pixel sizes, so the spec values at the end are derived from Fluent tokens.

## Twelve leaders share one ink baseline and differ by one signature feature each

The handwriting-first leaders have converged on a common core. Users treat it as the price of entry, not as a set of features. **Every leader's lasso selects, moves, resizes, deletes, and cuts, copies and pastes.** GoodNotes 6 sets the upper bound. It adds rotate, recolour, convert handwriting to text, take a screenshot of the region, and "add to Elements" (save the selection as a reusable sticker). It has freeform and rectangle modes, and tapping the lasso twice opens a type filter (handwriting, images, text) ([GoodNotes support](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000629656--GoodNotes-5-Select-content-for-copying-moving-etc-); [GoodNotes support](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001460175)). Its selection handles are clearly differentiated: "White corner squares: Scale; Blue circles: Resize; White protruding circle: Rotate" ([GoodNotes Lasso Tool](https://goodnotes-team.notion.site/Lasso-Tool-26fb740273d281008fc5edc2539b2b7b)). GoodNotes also has the most granular eraser. It offers **Stroke, Standard and Precision** erasers, an **Erase Filter** that can erase only highlighter, **Auto-Deselect** back to the previous tool, and **Scribble to Erase** ([GoodNotes support: Eraser](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/7353718249231-Erase-content-with-the-Eraser-tool); [Scribble to Erase](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/7443501398671-How-to-Use-Scribble-to-Erase)). Stroke stabilization arrived only in Q1 2025 ([GoodNotes blog](https://www.goodnotes.com/blog/features-fixes-updates-march-2025)). Undo/redo for page actions (add, delete, rotate, reorder, change template) arrived only in June 2025 ([GoodNotes blog](https://www.goodnotes.com/blog/features-fixes-updates-june-2025)). Both are a reminder that the market leader still ships basics late.

Page management follows the same reference model. GoodNotes's sidebar has a Pages tab with thumbnails and drag-reorder. It has a Bookmarks-only filter and an Outline tab that merges imported PDF outlines with custom entries ([GoodNotes support: Sidebar](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/9497798035983-Use-the-Sidebar-to-navigate-your-document)). Its Add Page menu offers **Before / After / Last Page** ([GoodNotes support](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/7353728016655)). Editable PDF export keeps hyperlinks and outlines ([GoodNotes support: Export](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/7353742824975-Export-documents-or-pages)). Noteshelf 3 competes on stationery, with "200+ exclusive templates", cover packs and configurable diaries ([App Store: Noteshelf 3](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/noteshelf-3-ai-digital-notes/id6458735203)). Notability offers plain, ruled, squared and dotted paper plus "My Templates" ([Colorado College guide](https://www.coloradocollege.edu/offices/accessibilityresources/images/quickstartguides/Notability%20Quick%20Start%20Guide.pdf)). Pen variety is a weaker differentiator than it looks. CollaNote advertises "over 25 different pens and brushes" ([App Store: CollaNote](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/collanote-notes-pdf-markup/id1540956268)), and ZoomNotes has eight pens including watercolour and italic ([ZoomNotes features](http://www.zoom-notes.com/?page_id=71)). The Windows-standard InkToolbar settles on just **Ballpoint, Pencil, Highlighter, Eraser, Ruler** ([Microsoft Learn: Inking controls](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-au/windows/uwp/ui-input/inking-controls)).

The PDF-annotation tools set a separate free baseline that a notebook which imports PDFs inherits automatically. **Adobe Acrobat Reader gives away annotate, fill forms and sign**. Its markup set covers sticky notes, typed text, highlight, underline, strikethrough and freehand. Signatures can be drawn, typed or uploaded as an image ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/blog/best-free-pdf-reader); [Trinity LibGuide](https://libguides.trinity.edu/digital-annotation/adobe); [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/online/pdf-editor.html)). Xodo's free tier adds an annotation summary list and grouped shape tools. Page organisation (split, merge, rotate, rearrange) plus flatten, compress and OCR is common, but usually paid ([Xodo tools](https://xodo.com/tools)). PDF-XChange Editor sets the power-user ceiling, with measurement, custom stamps, and snapping to grid and guides ([PDF-XChange manual](https://help.pdf-xchange.com/pdfxt11/features-overview.html)). One consequence follows. If a user can't highlight a sentence *snapped to the PDF's text* or sign a form, the app looks worse than free Reader, however good its ink is.

Canva supplies the vocabulary for the page-editor half. Its text controls cover font family, size by preset, typing or **+/− buttons**, colour, B/I/U/S, alignment (left, centre, right, justify), list, letter and line spacing, and **Effects** (shadow, glow, outline, background, splice, hollow, neon, curve) ([Canva Help: Format text](https://www.canva.com/help/format-text/); [Canva Help: Text effects](https://www.canva.com/help/text-effects/)). The shared element controls are **position, copy style, transparency, link, lock, duplicate, delete** ([PLU KB](https://kb.plu.edu/110125)). Position opens Arrange, Align, Tidy up and numeric W/H/X/Y/rotation fields ([Canva Help: Resize and crop](https://www.canva.com/help/resize-and-crop/)). Image controls are Crop (also on double-click), Flip and Transparency, plus an Edit side panel for filters and adjustments ([Canva Help: Photo editor](https://www.canva.com/help/image-editor/)). Canva itself warns that the toolbar "may look slightly different" as options are added. Only membership is sourced, so the order in the spec below is a synthesis.

## Users punish broken trust and laggy ports more than missing features

The strongest and most repeated theme in user sentiment is pricing betrayal, not missing features. **Notability went freemium in November 2021** and at first limited existing buyers to one year of the features they had paid for. It reversed course within days after the backlash ([AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/21/11/03/notability-backs-down-on-subscriptions-for-existing-users)). **GoodNotes 6 moved to a subscription in August 2023**, with a three-notebook free cap. Recent buyers "felt they got shortchanged", and some left for Noteful and Nebo ([Lemony Fizz](https://lemonyfizz.com/goodnotes-6-worth-it/); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2023/08/24/is-it-all-good-with-the-updated-goodnotes-6/87000)). GoodNotes restructured again in October 2025 around AI ([AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/10/goodnotes-update-brings-new-plans-ai-features-whiteboards-and-text-documents)). **Drawboard moved Pressure Ink behind its Pro subscription in March 2023**. It admits the change was unpopular and now distributes a "Legacy 2020 Version" ([Drawboard legacy](https://www.drawboard.com/legacy)). Its Trustpilot reviews complain that functionality people paid for was removed ([Trustpilot](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/www.drawboard.com?page=3)). The Drawboard case teaches the most: users see pressure-sensitive ink as basic quality, not a premium tier. AI draws a similar reaction. One reviewer refused to pay for GoodNotes 6 "until the AI features are optional so users can turn them off" ([Can Buy or Not](https://www.canbuyornot.com/reviews/goodnotes-6-review-price/)).

The second theme is the Windows port as a second-class citizen. GoodNotes's own forum has a thread titled **"Goodnotes for Windows is bad."** Users there report lag severe enough to need a restart, undo that reverts several actions at once, an unreliable lasso, delayed text, and a Surface pen eraser that "works only half the time" ([GoodNotes feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/950440-improve-goodnotes-for-android-windows-and-web/suggestions/47468507-goodnotes-for-windows-is-bad)). Users ask for a standalone program instead of a web app, and one calls the inability to view documents offline "ridiculous" ([GoodNotes feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/191274-customer-suggestions-for-goodnotes/suggestions/50442222-windows-web-app)). OneNote users have reported "several seconds for the pen strokes to appear". Microsoft rewrote the ink stack in 2022 for an **85% latency improvement**, and still advises turning off ink effects and handwriting conversion to reduce lag ([Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5230157/how-do-i-fix-the-input-lag-in-onenote)). Some Intel graphics drivers made it worse ([Intel Community](https://community.intel.com/t5/Graphics/Increased-delay-in-pen-strokes-using-Onenote/td-p/1316474)). Four specific failures make up "reliability" on Windows: input latency, an eraser end that works every time, undo that steps exactly one action, and a lasso that never misfires. An Electron app has to engineer around all four deliberately.

The third theme is lock-in and data loss. Samsung's October 2023 update "locked users out of their notes completely" on non-Galaxy PCs ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2023/10/03/samsung-notes-windows-galaxy-book/)). GoodNotes's native format "can only be opened in Goodnotes" ([GoodNotes support](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/7353742824975-Export-documents-or-pages)). Sync conflicts lose work across cloud-first apps. Acrobat users have lost "one hour or so of comments and highlights" to OneDrive conflict copies ([Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/acrobat-reader-mobile-discussions/problem-with-one-drive-sync/m-p/13894501/highlight/true)). **Export bloat is a long-standing, unsolved pain.** One GoodNotes user reports "a 14 mb pdf which became 84 mb after exportation", another an increase of "~800%". The requests have been open for years ([GoodNotes feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/191274-customer-suggestions-for-goodnotes-apple/suggestions/13494375-optimize-pdf-size-to-export)). GoodNotes also makes users choose. Its editable PDF keeps outlines and links but loses handwriting search. Its flattened PDF is searchable but drops outlines and links ([GoodNotes support](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/8537070839183-Differences-between-Editable-and-Flattened-PDF-Formats)).

Praise clusters around a short list. **Notability's time-stamped audio sync** is called "best-in-class" and "more intuitive" than GoodNotes's later version ([Paperlike](https://paperlike.com/blogs/paperlikers-insights/app-review-goodnotes-vs-notability)). GoodNotes's cross-page lasso is preferred over Notability's single-page lasso ([Affine](https://affine.pro/blog/goodnotes-vs-notability-tips)). One caveat: the sources conflict on whether GoodNotes has shipped cross-page moves outside vertical-scroll mode. Handwriting search is praised, Nebo's recognition was called "what Windows Ink should be" ([PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3105864/hands-on-myscript-nebo-is-what-windows-ink-should-be.html)), and Microsoft Journal's ink gestures (scratch out to delete, circle to select) earn praise too ([Windows Central](https://windowscentral.com/microsoft-video-shows-how-its-journal-app-isnt-just-onenote-clone)). Open-source Saber gets credit for a dark mode that inverts notes, images and PDFs, and for clean overlapping highlighter strokes ([Saber GitHub](https://github.com/hubortje/saber)). It is also a warning. One reviewer found that its undo doesn't undo erasing, that it has no cut/copy/paste, and that it sometimes deletes imported images. That reviewer called it usable "but just barely" ([AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/saber-handwritten-notes/about)). That's the fate of a feature-complete-on-paper app with an unreliable core.

## Windows lacks a native offline GoodNotes, and mouse users get no help

Taken together, the evidence describes one gap. Comparison sites still fall back to OneNote as the "safest default" on Surface, with Penbook, Bamboo Paper, Xodo, Journal, Stylus Labs Write and Saber as niche pen-first options ([crm.org](https://crm.org/news/best-goodnotes-alternatives)). None of them is a polished, page-based PDF notebook. **No incumbent offers all four of native Windows, fully offline, account-free, and one-time-priced.** That combination is the positioning. The feature list only needs to make it credible.

Mouse and trackpad users are a neglected segment the plan can serve on purpose. PDF Annotator automatically converts nearly straight strokes into exact lines and makes smoothing a per-tool toggle that works with a mouse ([PDF Annotator](https://www.pdfannotator.com/en/articles/auto_smooth)). Drawboard's smoothing toggle is "only available on devices with a touchscreen" ([Drawboard support](https://support.drawboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001578856-Drawboard-PDF-Pen-and-ink-settings)), which leaves mouse users out. Over-smoothing is just as harmful. A PDF-XChange user says smoothing "makes hand annotation unusable" at higher zoom, and the fix is a registry hack ([PDF-XChange Forum](https://forum.pdf-xchange.com/viewtopic.php?t=27912)). Adobe iPad users describe handwriting smoothed into spiky distortion ([Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-15/handwriting-annotations-smoothed-distorted-on-ipad-2292)). The planned "smoothing" item therefore needs three properties. Its strength should be adjustable. Its default should depend on the input device (strong for mouse, light or off for pen, which `PointerEvent.pointerType` makes trivial). And it should be measured in page units rather than screen pixels, so that zooming in doesn't distort strokes. Text-snapped highlighting from the PDF.js text layer matters for the same users, because a freehand highlighter dragged with a mouse never looks clean.

Interop is the last structural gap. The notes found no competitor that exports a PDF that is **flattened and searchable while keeping outlines and hyperlinks**, which GoodNotes explicitly can't do. Xodo treats flatten as a paid, explicit action ([Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9WZDNCRDJXP4)), which implies that the default export should keep annotations as real, editable PDF annotation objects. The planned zip project save is the right native format. It is open, local and backup-friendly. It should be paired with a PDF export that has an Editable/Flattened switch and a size/quality option that downsamples images. That pairing directly answers the 14→84 MB complaint. One uncertainty remains: no source covered importing native `.goodnotes` or `.one` files, so migration has to go through those apps' PDF exports.

## Professional polish means Fluent restraint, not decoration

Ink apps and design editors solve layout differently, and this app needs both. Ink-first apps use **one horizontal tool strip across the top, with the active tool's swatches and sizes shown inline to its right**. GoodNotes's pen shows "a preset stroke width and type, and color on the right-hand side" ([GoodNotes Pen Tool](https://goodnotes-team.notion.site/Pen-Tool-26fb740273d2818dbb55fe352d8fa431)). Journal and OneNote's Pen Focused View follow the same model ([Microsoft Support: Journal](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/journal/pen-toolbar); [Microsoft Insider](https://insider.office.com/en-us/blog/pen-focused-view-in-onenote-on-windows)). Drawboard's Markup Toolbar "sits at the top of the canvas and can be docked or floated" ([Drawboard support](https://support.drawboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/15630133419279-Drawboard-PDF-Using-the-Markup-Toolbar-on-Windows)). Notability lets the toolbox snap to any of four sides ([La Salle Falconer](https://lasallefalconer.com/2024/01/mastering-notability-tips-and-tricks-for-ultimate-note-taking-success)). GoodNotes users ask for the same ([feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/191274-customer-suggestions-for-goodnotes/suggestions/44791201-moveable-tool-bar-i-e-ability-to-put-the-tool-ba)). That makes a fixed top bar the right default and docking a later enhancement. Design editors add a **contextual bar that follows the selection**. Windows has the matching control. **CommandBarFlyout** is "the recommended control for creating context menus". It shows collapsed and without taking focus when triggered by a selection, and expanded on right-click ([windows-dev-docs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/docs/hub/apps/develop/ui/controls/command-bar-flyout.md)). CommandBar moves low-priority commands into a "…" overflow as width shrinks, which gives a principled rule for narrow windows ([windows-dev-docs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/docs/hub/apps/develop/ui/controls/command-bar.md)).

The pen picker convention is consistent across GoodNotes, Drawboard, Journal and WinUI. **Selecting a tool shows a few colour slots and sizes inline. Clicking the active tool or slot again opens the full settings.** GoodNotes allows **up to 12 colour slots and at least 3**, and tapping a slot again opens a palette with colour wheel, HEX, eyedropper and "Add to Presets" ([GoodNotes Pen Tool](https://goodnotes-team.notion.site/Pen-Tool-26fb740273d2818dbb55fe352d8fa431)). Drawboard opens customisation on a second click and adds presets with "+" ([Drawboard blog](https://www.drawboard.com/blog/how-to-draw-on-a-pdf-with-drawboard-pdf)). Users keep asking GoodNotes for combined pen+colour+thickness favourites shown permanently in the toolbar ([GoodNotes feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/191274-customer-suggestions-for-goodnotes/suggestions/38727190-have-more-preset-colors-and-thicknesses-displayed)), so a preset row is an inexpensive way to beat the leader. Journal resets pen settings per journal unless "Remember Pen State" is on ([Office Watch](https://office-watch.com/2022/journal-ink-app-microsoft/)). Persisting them by default is the friendlier choice. The page sidebar has the clearest negative evidence. GoodNotes Windows users complain that thumbnails are "too small to make out details" and that the sidebar can't be resized, and they ask for a persistent sidebar, space-to-pan, Ctrl+wheel zoom, and typing a page number to jump ([GoodNotes Windows feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/950440-how-can-we-improve-goodnotes-for-windows/filters/hot?page=69)). Pull-to-add-page duplicating the last page instead of adding a blank one is another named irritation ([GoodNotes feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/191274-customer-suggestions-for-goodnotes/suggestions/45594754-pull-to-add-new-page-rather-than-duplicate-last-pa)).

Visual professionalism on Windows 11 is well specified and mostly comes down to discipline. Use **8px radius for windows, flyouts and dialogs and 4px for controls** ([windows-dev-docs: Geometry](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/docs/hub/apps/design/signature-experiences/geometry.md)). Use **Segoe UI Variable** on a fixed ramp of Caption 12/16, Body 14/20, Subtitle 20/28 and Title 28/36, with sentence case everywhere ([windows-dev-docs: Typography](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/docs/hub/apps/design/signature-experiences/typography.md)). Use Mica for the base layer and **acrylic "only for transient, light-dismiss surfaces"**. Content panes should be opaque ([Materials](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/docs/hub/apps/design/signature-experiences/materials.md); [Acrylic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/docs/hub/apps/design/style/acrylic.md)). Use shadows "purposefully — rather than decoratively" ([Layering](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/docs/hub/apps/design/signature-experiences/layering.md)). Accent colour is "used sparingly", and only one colour signals interactivity ([Color](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/docs/hub/apps/design/signature-experiences/color.md)). Exact colour values are available from WinUI's theme XAML and the Fluent 2 token source ([microsoft-ui-xaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/main/controls/dev/CommonStyles/Common_themeresources_any.xaml); [fluentui tokens](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/fluentui/master/packages/tokens/src/global/brandColors.ts)). The `@fluentui/svg-icons` set has 20px regular icons for eraser, highlight, hand, crop, flip, undo and copy, all themeable through `currentColor` ([fluentui-system-icons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/fluentui-system-icons/main/packages/svg-icons/README.md)). The "generic AI template" look is the inverse of this list. Practitioners' pieces (opinion, not studies) name Inter on white, **purple-to-indigo gradients**, emoji as icons, glow and gradient blobs, and three rounded cards ([prg.sh](https://prg.sh/ramblings/Why-Your-AI-Keeps-Building-the-Same-Purple-Gradient-Website); [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated)). For snapping and measurement, design tools use one saturated colour reserved for guides. Figma's measurement line is red and can't be changed ([Figma Help](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039956974)), and Canva's guides are pink ([Blogging Guide](https://bloggingguide.com/how-to-use-rulers-and-guides-in-canva/)).

## Conclusion

The research changes where this app should invest. The planned list reads as a strong Canva-style page editor with ink attached. The market rewards the opposite emphasis: **ink and PDF interaction depth first, design-editor polish second**. The cheapest high-impact additions sit next to work already planned. Extend the eraser into three modes plus a filter. Extend selection into a lasso with type filter and recolour. Extend pen settings into a preset row. Extend export into editable or flattened output with compression. Extend page operations into a resizable sidebar with insert-between and change-template. None of these needs AI or a network, and together they close most of the distance to GoodNotes on iPad. That distance is exactly what Windows users say they lack.

The second implication is that trust is a feature here. With three category leaders burned by paywall reversals, a Windows-native app that is offline, account-free, stores an open zip/PDF format, never paywalls ink quality, and keeps recognition opt-in and on-device holds a position incumbents can't copy without undoing their business models. Audio-synced ink, which is offline-capable and the most praised single feature, is the clearest differentiator to build after the baseline. Recognition (OCR search, convert to text) should follow only when it can be done well locally. Done badly, it repeats the "make AI optional" backlash.

### Main toolbar order (centre zone)

**Select (V) · Lasso (L) · Pen (P) · Highlighter (H) · Eraser (E) · Text (T) · Shapes (S) · Image (I) · Sticky note · Hand** │ **contextual cluster**. The contextual cluster shows the active tool's options inline. For pen and highlighter it holds the preset slots, then 3 colour swatches, then 3 thickness dots. For eraser it holds a mode segmented control (Stroke / Partial / Precision) and a filter menu. For shapes it holds the shape-type picker (rectangle, ellipse, line, arrow, triangle) plus fill and stroke. For text it holds "Add heading / subheading / body" and the saved text styles. Tool buttons are 40×40 with 20px Fluent *regular* icons, and the selected tool switches to the *filled* icon. The selected state is a subtle fill (`--fill-subtle-hover`) plus an accent icon, and pen and highlighter buttons carry a **3px underline in their current ink colour**. Each tooltip names the tool and its shortcut, e.g. "Pen (P)", after ~500ms.

### Selection and contextual toolbars

The selection visuals use a 1px solid accent bounding box that is always 1 screen px regardless of zoom. Handles are 8 white 8×8 squares with a 1px accent border and a 16px hit area, and edge handles hide below 24px. The rotate handle is a 10px circle on a 1px stem 20px above top-centre. The lasso path is a 1px dashed (4/4) accent line with animated marching ants and a 6% accent fill. Snap guides and distance labels use **one reserved guide colour** that appears nowhere else in the UI. Use magenta `#E5007E` (Canva-like) or red `#F24822` (Figma-like). Distance labels are pills with a 2px radius, 11px semibold white text and 2×4px padding. The floating bar fades in over 83–167ms after pointer-up, never during a drag. Right-click opens the same commands expanded as a vertical menu (CommandBarFlyout reactive mode). Each selection type gets the bar order below, and every bar ends with "…" for overflow.

| Selection type | Floating bar order (│ = divider) |
|---|---|
| Ink (lasso) | Copy · Duplicate · Delete │ Colour · Thickness · Convert to text (when recognition is enabled) │ Screenshot · Add to elements │ … (Flip, Bring forward/back, Move to page…) |
| Text box | Font family (160px, previews in its own face, recents + search) · Size `[−][16][+]` · Text colour "A" with 4px colour bar · B · I · U · S · Align (click cycles L→C→R→J) · List (cycles none→bullet→number) · Spacing popover │ Text style ▾ (saved styles) · Effects │ … |

| Any object (shared tail) | Position (arrange, align, distribute, X/Y/W/H/rotation) · Lock · Duplicate · Delete |

### Page sidebar

The header is 40px tall and shows "Pages" (14px semibold), the page count, a Pages / Outline toggle and "+". Each thumbnail fills the column width minus 32px (≈156×202px at 220px width), on a white card with a 2px radius, 1px `--stroke-card` and shadow2, with 16px vertical gaps. The page number sits centred below in 12px secondary text. The current page gets a **2px accent outline with 2px offset**, and its number turns accent and semibold. Hover adds a subtle 4px-radius fill. Hovering the gap between thumbnails shows a 2px accent line with a 20px circular "+" that inserts a blank page at that point. A blank "+ Add page" ghost card closes the list, and it never duplicates the last page. During a drag-reorder the thumbnail lifts (shadow16, 0.9 opacity, scale 1.02), a 2px accent insertion line marks the drop point, and the list auto-scrolls near the edges. Multi-select uses Ctrl/Shift+click. The context menu (right-click or hover "…", 32px rows, 8px radius) has four groups: Add page before, Add page after, Duplicate │ Change template…, Rotate │ Bookmark, Copy, Export page… │ **Delete** (the only red text). Delete shows an "Undo" toast. Dropping a lasso selection onto a thumbnail moves it to that page (D3).
